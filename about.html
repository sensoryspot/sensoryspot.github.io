About Sensory SpOT Studio

Pediatric OT experience, shaped into practical resources.

Sensory SpOT Studio brings together occupational therapy experience, hands-on learning, and materials designed for the realities of classrooms, therapy sessions, and home routines.

Professional background

A thoughtful, real-world perspective.

The creator of Sensory SpOT Studio is a pediatric occupational therapist with experience in both outpatient pediatric clinics and school-based occupational therapy.

She earned a Master’s degree in Occupational Therapy from the University of New Hampshire in 2017. Her training also includes a specialized interest and coursework in sensory processing and participation in the interdisciplinary LEND program.

Working across pediatric settings has reinforced the value of clear instructions, flexible activities, and collaboration with the educators, therapists, and families who support children every day.

Experience that informs the work

From clinical practice to everyday learning.

Outpatient pediatrics

Experience in outpatient pediatric clinics provided a foundation in child-centered occupational therapy and working alongside families.

School-based practice

School-based occupational therapy brought that perspective into classrooms, shared routines, educational teams, and the practical rhythm of a school day.

Resource creation

Sensory SpOT Studio turns that experience into approachable sensory, fine motor, handwriting, movement, and learning materials.

The approach

Purposeful play, without overpromising.

Resources are created to offer practical educational activities—not diagnoses, guaranteed outcomes, or individualized treatment.

Clear

Directions should be understandable for the adult preparing or guiding an activity.

Engaging

Hands-on materials can make practice inviting while keeping the learning purpose visible.

Adaptable

Activities should leave room for adults to adjust materials, pace, and participation.

Grounded

Ideas are considered in the context of real classrooms, therapy sessions, and family routines.

Educational information

A clear professional boundary.

Sensory SpOT Studio resources and guides provide general educational ideas. They are not a substitute for individualized occupational therapy, medical advice, assessment, diagnosis, or treatment.

Adults should use their knowledge of the child, the environment, and applicable school or organizational policies when selecting and adapting an activity.
