Resources from Sensory SpOT

Practical ideas, ready for everyday routines.

Explore approachable materials designed to help educators, school professionals, and families include sensory-support opportunities throughout the day.

What you can expect

Resources made with real settings in mind.

Materials from Sensory SpOT focus on clear directions, engaging formats, and ideas adults can adapt to the routines around them.

Regulation activities

Short, engaging opportunities that can be incorporated into transitions, breaks, and familiar parts of a student's day.

Classroom routines and supports

Practical tools and strategies created with classroom environments, full schedules, and everyday participation in mind.

Visual and printable resources

Ready-to-use materials that make supportive ideas easier to see, explain, and revisit as part of normal routines.

Guidance for educators and families

Approachable information for adults who want practical sensory-support ideas they can understand and use with care.
